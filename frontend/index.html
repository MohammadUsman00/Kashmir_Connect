World-class crafts, premium saffron, breathtaking tourism — yet most businesses have zero digital presence, no direct-to-buyer channel, and no way to prove authenticity.

🏺

No online presence

A carpet weaver earning ₹8,000/month while Delhi middlemen earn ₹80,000 on the same carpet. The artisan has no way to reach buyers directly.
